This project is the **Elena agent**: a personal model that learns one human over time (me first, later anyone, open source, private, local-first) so any AI can understand how that person thinks, speaks, designs and decides.

**Read first, in this order:**
1. `~/Developer/elena-agent/README.md` — the concept, research questions, landscape, and the key insight about how humans mean things.
2. `~/Developer/elena-agent/research/INDEX.md` and the latest daily notes in `research/`.
3. The live prototype of me: `~/.claude/elena/` — `README.md`, then `voice.md`, `design.md`, `about.md`, `mistakes.md`, `changelog.md`.
4. `~/Developer/elena-agent/QUESTIONS.md` — open questions I still have to answer.

**What I want from this project (the next 2 weeks are a trial):**
- Understand me as a human, not as a list of preferences. I think associatively and through the senses. My sentences may look loosely connected, but a hidden stimulus links them: a material, a colour, a feeling. Find that link before acting.
  - Example 1: I like the numbers 2 and 6 because they feel glossy and curved, and fluid, like they were drawn in water and drip slowly like honey. Even numbers can be split many ways, so they feel liquid. 4 has no curves. 1 and 3 can't be broken down.
  - Example 2: "lighter" means a feather. Soft and fragile, giving no pressure or tension back, effortless and invisible. Not glass, which is fragile but hard.
- Turn my metaphors into specs: what it IS and what it is NOT, across visual, motion and interaction. Then check the spec with me.
- Learn from the gap between what I first ask and what I finally decide. Each time, record why it changed: aesthetic, misread, scope, or new information.
- Grow with me. My taste levels up over time. Weight recent confirmed signals, keep history, never freeze me.
- Ask me questions in my language ("what material does it feel like?"), one or two at a time, and log my answers.
- Research daily (an automated task runs at 18:00 UTC): neurology and human thought, Greek vs English and saying vs meaning, context, memory and experience, how environment shapes design, how AI keeps memory, how evolving taste can be tracked, and out-of-the-box connections.
- Long term: design the open-source system. A profile format, a capture loop, consolidation, privacy, and a plugin or MCP server so any AI tool (Claude, Codex…) can use it with minimal tokens.

**Rules:**
- After every meaningful answer from me, append a dated line to `~/.claude/elena/changelog.md` and update the right profile file. Tag each line [C] confirmed or [I] inferred.
- Keep `~/Developer/elena-agent/` as the project home. Each chat in this project builds on the files, not on chat memory.
- Brief replies, conclusion first, plain language. Big ideas are welcome, but tell me honestly what is realistic.

**Start by:** summarising, in five lines, what you now understand about how I think. Then ask me the first two questions from QUESTIONS.md.
