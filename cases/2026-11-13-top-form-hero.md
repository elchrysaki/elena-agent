# Case: Top Form City home hero (2026-11-13) — from first ask to "love it"

A full design convergence in one day. Useful for the agent because the path shows how Elena's taste resolves when
several references pull in different directions.

## The path (first ask → final decision)

## The style she approved (Design B)
1. **One dominant real action photo, vibrant.** Saturation up, only a light edge shade. Darkening a photo to fit text reads as "murky", the opposite of vibrant.
2. **Depth by layering: subject in front of a giant word.** The word lives *inside* the scene (behind the swimmer, in the water). This is the third time she has loved this pattern: vita-travel (word behind a mountain), UniTravel C ("Connect" between sky and city), Top Form D. → [C] a signature of her taste.
3. **The type hero is the brand name, giant, wide, uppercase, one piece** — not a slogan, not a staircase of words.
4. **Strict 3-level hierarchy:** L1 the word + subject, L2 one short line, L3 one primary button. Extras only if functional (a small live-status glass chip).
5. **Apple materials:** thin translucent glass header, the photo showing through; exactly one accent-coloured primary action per view.
6. **Identity from the client's own symbol** (their logo is a fist rising out of water → a swimmer bursting through the brand word), not from a template.
7. **Motion:** honey-smooth, moving with the scroll; the word rises as one piece on load.

## What the agent should learn (generalisable)
- **Converge by elimination, not by adding:** every rejected preview removed a dimension (editorial type, calm subject, template structure, slogans) until one idea with one focal moment remained.
- **References are for a single property**, not a whole layout: chanpan = cut-out subject + wordmark; Rick Allan = hierarchy; Ascent = clarity; UniTravel A/C = big type + image + layering. The winning design used one property from each that fit the goals.
- **Self-critique table before showing:** structure, layers, hierarchy, colour/vibrancy, goals, each checked against the named skills (Apple, Awwwards). She asked for this explicitly; do it by default.
- **Cost awareness:** she wants cheap static previews (one desktop image) before any code; building first wastes her tokens.
- **Audience overrides personal taste, but not energy:** a gym gets strong and vibrant; calm goes into motion quality.
